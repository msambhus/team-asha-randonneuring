# Live Tracking — Rider Guide

Share your location with the club during a ride so everyone can follow along on the
ride's **Live Map**. You set this up **on the ride's own live map**, and there are two
ways to appear — pick one:

| | ⌚ **Garmin LiveTrack** | 📱 **Phone beacon** |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | One-time app setup, then a fresh link each ride | None — one tap |
| Works with screen off? | **Yes** (recommended for long brevets) | **No** — screen must stay on |
| Source on map | ⌚ solid ring | 📱 dashed ring |
| Best for | The actual ride | Quick tests / short stretches |

---

## Before you start (both methods)

1. **Log in** to the club site — only logged-in club members can see the live map.
2. **Complete your rider profile** (needed to share or view).
3. **Mark yourself "Going"** on the ride. *Only riders who are Going show up on that
   ride's map.*
4. Make sure **live tracking is enabled** once (your master switch): **Live → Live
   tracking settings → Enable**. You can turn it off there any time.

Then open the ride you're riding: **Live** (top menu) → under *"Set up & view a ride's
live map"*, tap your ride → its **Live Map**. At the top you'll see
**"Appear on this map — pick how you'll share"** with the two options below.

> 🔑 **Tracking is per-ride.** What you set up on a ride's map only shows on *that* ride.
> You won't accidentally appear on another ride's map.

---

## ⌚ Option 1 — Garmin LiveTrack (screen-off, recommended)

This relays your watch/Edge GPS through the Garmin Connect app on your phone, so it keeps
working with your phone locked in your pocket.

### One-time setup (in the Garmin Connect mobile app)
1. Open **Garmin Connect** → **More → LiveTrack** (sometimes under *Safety & Tracking*).
2. Turn on **LiveTrack** and enable **Auto Start** so a session begins automatically when
   you start an activity. *(Exact menu names vary slightly by app version.)*

### Each ride
1. **Start your ride/activity** on your Garmin so a LiveTrack session is live.
2. In Garmin Connect, open the active LiveTrack session and **copy the share link** — it
   looks like `https://livetrack.garmin.com/session/…/token/…`.
3. On the **ride's Live Map**, open the **⌚ Garmin LiveTrack** card, **paste the link**,
   and tap **Save link**.

> ⚠️ **The Garmin link is new every ride.** Garmin creates a fresh session (new link) each
> time you start an activity. The old link dies, so **paste a fresh link on the ride's map
> at the start of every ride** — otherwise you won't appear. (Stop & restart the activity =
> a new link too.) To stop early, tap **Stop tracking this ride** on the same card.

You'll show up on the map within a few minutes (the server polls Garmin every ~5 min).

---

## 📱 Option 2 — Phone beacon (screen-on)

No setup — but your phone has to stay awake, and you start it from the ride's map.

1. On the ride's **Live Map**, open the **📱 Share from this phone** card.
2. Tap **Share my location** and allow location access.
3. **Keep the page open with your screen on.** Phone browsers can't share location in the
   background, so if the screen sleeps or you switch apps, sharing pauses.
4. Optional: turn on **Low power** (coarser GPS, updates every 60s) to save battery.
5. Tap **Stop sharing** any time.

> ⚠️ Great for a quick check or a short controlled stretch, but **not practical for a full
> brevet** because the screen must stay on. Use Garmin LiveTrack for the real ride.

---

## What everyone sees & the caveats

- **Who can see it:** only logged-in club members, on that ride's Live Map.
- **Per-ride:** your dot only shows on the ride you set up — never bleeds into other rides.
- **Privacy / retention:** your location history is **automatically deleted after 7 days**,
  and you can turn tracking **off any time** in Live tracking settings.
- **"Stale" dots:** if your last point is more than ~10 minutes old, your marker fades —
  usually means signal dropped, screen slept (beacon), or the Garmin session ended.
- **The ⏸ / 🚴 badge** on your dot is your live movement state from GPS speed:
  ⏸ stopped · 🚶 walking · 🚴 riding · 🚗 driving. ⏸ just means you're not moving right now.
- **Off-route riders still show:** if you're more than ~800 m from the route line, your dot
  still appears, but route-specific stats (distance-to-go, climb left, headwind) are hidden.
- **You must be "Going"** on the ride to appear.
- **Garmin link is per-ride** (see the warning above) — the #1 reason someone "isn't showing."

---

### Quick troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely cause / fix |
|---|---|
| I enabled Garmin but don't appear | Link is stale — start the activity, copy a **fresh** LiveTrack link, re-save it on the ride's map. |
| I appeared, then went stale | Garmin session ended, or (beacon) the screen slept. |
| I'm not on the map at all | Not marked **Going** on that ride, master tracking off, or profile incomplete. |
| Beacon keeps pausing | Screen turned off / app backgrounded — keep the page open, screen on. |
| My old points vanished after the update | Tracking is now per-ride — re-link Garmin (or re-share) on the ride's own map. |
