Live Tracking

Share your live location with the club during a ride using Garmin LiveTrack — it broadcasts your watch/bike-computer GPS through the Garmin Connect app on your phone, so it keeps working with your screen off and your phone in your pocket. Only logged-in club members can see the live map, position history is automatically deleted after 7 days, and you can turn this off at any time.

⚠️ You need a fresh link for every ride. Garmin creates a brand-new LiveTrack session — and a new link — each time you start an activity. The previous link stops working, so grab a new one and paste it below at the start of each ride.
One-time setup (in the Garmin Connect app)

You only do this once on your phone.

  1. Open the Garmin Connect app and go to More → LiveTrack. On some versions it's under Safety & Tracking → LiveTrack. Menu names vary by app version.
  2. Turn LiveTrack on, and enable Auto Start so a session begins automatically whenever you start an activity.
Each ride
  1. Start your ride/activity on your Garmin so a LiveTrack session goes live.
  2. In Garmin Connect, open the active LiveTrack session and copy the share link. It looks like https://livetrack.garmin.com/session/…/token/…
  3. Paste it below, tick Enable live tracking, and tap Save settings.
